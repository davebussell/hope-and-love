EMDR Therapy

EMDR Therapy in Belleville & the Quinte Region

EMDR therapy for Belleville and the Quinte region, available by secure video across Ontario, with in-person sessions nearby in Prince Edward County. Evidence-based support for trauma, anxiety, and the experiences that still weigh on the present.

If you are in Belleville, Quinte West, CFB Trenton, Napanee, or the wider Quinte region and looking for EMDR therapy, Hope & Love offers secure virtual EMDR province-wide, with in-person sessions available a short drive away in Prince Edward County. You work with Matt Harris, a Registered Psychotherapist who is EMDR Certified (EMDRIA approved).

EMDR for the Quinte community

EMDR can help with trauma and PTSD, anxiety and panic, and the distressing memories and beliefs that keep the past alive in the present. For members of the Forces community around CFB Trenton, and for first responders, EMDR is a well-researched option for operational and post-traumatic stress.

  • Trauma and PTSD, including operational and service-related stress
  • Anxiety, panic, and phobias
  • Childhood and complex trauma
  • Grief and distressing memories

Virtual across Ontario, in person in the County

Most clients in the Belleville and Quinte area meet with us by secure video, which reaches everywhere in Ontario. If you would prefer to meet face to face, in-person EMDR is available in Prince Edward County. You can also explore our wider therapy for Belleville and the Quinte region.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is EMDR therapy available in Belleville?
Yes. We offer EMDR for the Belleville and Quinte region by secure video across Ontario, with in-person sessions available nearby in Prince Edward County.
Do you offer in-person EMDR near Belleville?
Yes. In-person EMDR is available in Prince Edward County, a short drive from Belleville. Virtual EMDR is available everywhere in Ontario.
Can EMDR help with military or first-responder trauma?
Yes. EMDR is well researched for post-traumatic and operational stress, which can matter for the CFB Trenton and first-responder community.
What does EMDR help with?
Trauma and PTSD, anxiety and panic, phobias, grief, and the beliefs that outlast difficult experiences. Learn more on our EMDR therapy page.
Is virtual EMDR as effective as in person?
For many people, yes. See online EMDR for how it works over secure video.

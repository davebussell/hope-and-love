Virtual Therapy for All of Ontario

Secure video sessions with Matt Harris, Registered Psychotherapist and EMDR-certified, available to anyone in Ontario. Specialized support for trauma and PTSD, anxiety, depression, addiction, and relationships - from wherever you are most comfortable.

Anywhere in Ontario Secure Video Sessions EMDR Certified · Registered Psychotherapist

Good therapy should not depend on your postal code. Many people in Ontario live hours from a specialist, cannot fit a commute around work and family, or simply prefer the privacy of talking from home. Virtual therapy removes those barriers: the same evidence-based work, the same therapist, with no waiting room and no drive.

Sessions take place over a secure, encrypted video platform. You need a private space, a phone, tablet, or computer, and a reasonable internet connection. Everything else - including the free consultation - works exactly as it would in person.

Our focus: EMDR and trauma-informed therapy

Hope & Love’s primary focus is EMDR therapy (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing), an evidence-based approach that helps the brain reprocess painful experiences so they stop intruding on the present. Matt is EMDR-certified (EMDRIA approved), and EMDR works well online - research and clinical experience both support virtual delivery.

  • EMDR for trauma & PTSD - single-incident and complex trauma, first responders, accidents, loss
  • EMDR for anxiety - panic, phobias, performance anxiety, and fear that will not reason away
  • EMDR for depression - when low mood is rooted in experiences that never resolved
  • EMDR for addiction - addressing the pain underneath compulsive patterns

Virtual counselling services across Ontario

  • Anxiety therapy - persistent worry, panic attacks, social anxiety, OCD
  • Depression therapy - low mood, burnout, grief, postpartum depression, loneliness
  • Trauma & PTSD therapy - EMDR, IFS, and trauma-informed care

Who virtual therapy works well for

  • People in smaller communities or rural Ontario who want specialized care without the travel

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is virtual therapy as effective as in-person?
For most concerns, yes. Research on video-delivered psychotherapy, including EMDR, shows outcomes comparable to in-person care. What matters most is the fit with your therapist and consistent attendance - both of which are often easier online.
Can I do EMDR online?
Yes. EMDR adapts well to video using on-screen bilateral stimulation or self-tapping guided by Matt. Your free consultation is a good place to ask whether it suits your situation.
Where in Ontario can I book from?
Anywhere in the province. Matt is registered with the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario and can see clients located anywhere in Ontario by secure video.
What do I need for a session?
A private, quiet space; a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera; and a stable internet connection. A link is sent before each appointment - nothing to install.
Does insurance cover virtual therapy?
Many Ontario extended health benefit plans cover sessions with a Registered Psychotherapist, virtual included. Matt provides detailed receipts for insurance submission. Check your plan for "psychotherapy" or "registered psychotherapist" coverage.

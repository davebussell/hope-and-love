EMDR for Depression

Depression can be held in place by experiences that were never fully processed, by loss, by shame, or by long-held beliefs about not being enough. Where that is the case, EMDR can help address what keeps the low mood stuck.

Depression is complex, and no single approach fits everyone. EMDR is best established as a therapy for trauma, but it can also help with depression when low mood is connected to unprocessed experiences, grief, or painful beliefs about yourself. Often it is used alongside other support rather than on its own.

When EMDR may help with depression

  • Depression that followed loss, trauma, or a painful life event
  • Low mood held in place by shame or harsh self-criticism
  • Negative core beliefs, such as "I am not enough" or "nothing will change"
  • Grief that has become stuck
  • Depression that sits alongside anxiety or past trauma

How EMDR approaches depression

With EMDR, we identify the experiences and beliefs that feed the depression and use bilateral stimulation to help the brain reprocess them. As those memories settle, the beliefs attached to them can loosen, which can make room for a steadier, more hopeful sense of yourself.

Because depression has many causes, we start with a careful conversation about what is going on for you, then build a plan that may combine EMDR with other support. If you are in crisis, please see the safety note below.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can EMDR treat depression?
EMDR is best established for trauma, but it can help with depression when the low mood is rooted in unprocessed experiences, loss, or painful beliefs. It is often used alongside other support rather than on its own.
How does EMDR help with low mood?
By helping the brain reprocess the experiences and beliefs that feed the depression, so they carry less weight in day-to-day life.
Is EMDR used on its own for depression?
Not usually. For depression, EMDR is often one part of a wider plan. We decide together what is most useful for you.
How many sessions does it take?
It varies with what is driving the depression. We set goals early and review progress together as we go.
Is EMDR for depression available online?
Yes. Online EMDR is available across Ontario by secure video.

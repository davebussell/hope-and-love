EMDR Therapy in Prince Edward County

In-person and virtual EMDR therapy in Prince Edward County. Evidence-based reprocessing for trauma, anxiety, and the experiences that still shape the present, with an EMDR Certified therapist in the County.

Hope & Love offers EMDR therapy in person in Prince Edward County, serving Picton, Wellington, Bloomfield, and the surrounding area, as well as by secure video across Ontario. You work with Matt Harris, a Registered Psychotherapist who is EMDR Certified (EMDRIA approved).

EMDR in the County

EMDR is an evidence-based therapy that helps the brain reprocess overwhelming experiences, so they settle into the past instead of returning as flashbacks, anxiety, or a constant sense of being on guard. It does not require you to describe every detail of what happened, and the work is carefully paced.

  • Trauma and PTSD, including childhood and complex trauma
  • Anxiety, panic, and phobias
  • Grief and distressing memories
  • Negative beliefs that outlast the events that created them

In person or by video

Prince Edward County is our in-person home, so you can meet face to face for EMDR here. If video is easier, virtual EMDR reaches everywhere in Ontario, including nearby Belleville and the Quinte region. You can also explore our wider therapy in Prince Edward County.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer in-person EMDR in Prince Edward County?
Yes. Prince Edward County is our in-person location, serving Picton, Wellington, Bloomfield, and the surrounding area. Virtual EMDR is also available across Ontario.
What is EMDR?
EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is an evidence-based therapy that helps the brain reprocess distressing memories. Learn more on our EMDR therapy page.
How many sessions will I need?
It varies. A single recent event may take a handful of sessions, while complex trauma usually takes longer. We review progress together as we go.
Is EMDR covered by insurance?
Many Ontario extended health plans cover sessions with a Registered Psychotherapist. We provide detailed receipts for you to submit to your insurer.
Can I switch between in-person and virtual?
Yes. Many clients mix in-person sessions in the County with virtual sessions when that is easier.

IFS (Internal Family Systems)

IFS, or parts work, is a gentle, evidence-informed approach that helps you understand the different parts of yourself with curiosity and compassion, rather than judgment.

Internal Family Systems (IFS), often called parts work, is a gentle and increasingly evidence-informed approach. It helps you understand the different parts of yourself, the protector that keeps you busy, the critic, the part that feels young and hurt, with curiosity and compassion rather than judgment.

What IFS can help with

  • Trauma and the parts that formed to cope with it
  • Shame, self-criticism, and inner conflict
  • Anxiety and overwhelm
  • Addiction and compulsive patterns
  • Feeling at war with yourself

How IFS works

IFS starts from a simple idea: there are no bad parts. Even the parts that cause difficulty are usually trying to protect you. As you get to know them with compassion, and lead from a calmer, steadier core sometimes called the Self, the parts can relax and heal, and the inner conflict can ease.

IFS works well alongside EMDR for trauma and is often helpful in trauma therapy and addiction therapy, where shame and protective patterns run deep.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is IFS?
Internal Family Systems is a compassionate, parts-based approach to therapy. It helps you understand and work with the different parts of yourself, led from a calmer, steadier core.
What does "parts work" mean?
Parts work means relating to the different sides of yourself, such as a protector, a critic, or a hurt younger part, with curiosity and compassion, so they can settle and heal.
What does IFS help with?
IFS can help with trauma, shame, self-criticism, anxiety, inner conflict, and compulsive patterns, among other concerns.
Is IFS evidence-based?
IFS has a growing evidence base and is increasingly used for trauma and related concerns. Results vary by person, so we set goals together and review progress.
How is IFS different from EMDR?
IFS works with your inner parts and their relationships, while EMDR reprocesses specific memories. They complement each other, and we can combine them.
Is IFS available online in Ontario?
Yes. IFS is available by secure video across Ontario, as well as in person.

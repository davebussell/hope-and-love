CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy)

CBT is a practical, evidence-based therapy that helps you notice and shift the thoughts and behaviours that keep you stuck, so you can respond to life differently.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is one of the most researched and widely used therapies. It is practical and focused: we look at the links between your thoughts, feelings, and behaviours, and build concrete skills to change the patterns that keep you stuck.

What CBT can help with

  • Anxiety, worry, and panic
  • Depression and low mood
  • Unhelpful thinking patterns and harsh self-talk
  • Stress, sleep difficulties, and avoidance
  • Low self-worth and perfectionism

How CBT works

Thoughts, feelings, and behaviours are closely connected. CBT helps you notice the thoughts and habits that keep difficult feelings going, test them gently, and practise new responses. It is collaborative and skills-based, and some of the most useful work happens between sessions, in everyday life.

CBT pairs well with other approaches. Where a concern is rooted in past experiences, we may combine it with EMDR or parts work (IFS). For anxiety and depression specifically, see anxiety therapy and depression therapy.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is CBT?
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy is a practical, evidence-based approach that works with the links between thoughts, feelings, and behaviours to help you change patterns that keep you stuck.
What does CBT help with?
CBT is well researched for anxiety, depression, panic, stress, sleep, and unhelpful thinking patterns, among other concerns.
How does CBT work?
We identify the thoughts and habits that keep difficult feelings going, test them gently, and practise new responses. It is collaborative and skills-based, with some work happening between sessions.
How many CBT sessions will I need?
CBT is often shorter-term and focused, though it varies with what you are working on. We set goals early and review progress together.
How is CBT different from EMDR?
CBT works with present-day thoughts and behaviours, while EMDR works with how past experiences are stored. Many people benefit from both, and we can combine them.
Is CBT available online in Ontario?
Yes. CBT is available by secure video across Ontario, as well as in person.

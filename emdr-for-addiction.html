EMDR Therapy

EMDR for Addiction & Recovery

Compulsive patterns often sit on top of pain the mind has not been able to process. Used alongside addiction therapy, EMDR can help address the trauma, triggers, and shame that so often drive addiction.

For many people, addiction is tangled up with unprocessed experiences. Substances and compulsive behaviours can become a way to manage pain, memories, or feelings that feel unbearable. EMDR works with that underlying material, which is one reason it is increasingly used as part of addiction therapy.

How EMDR can support recovery

  • Reprocessing trauma that sits underneath compulsive patterns
  • Reducing the charge of triggers and cravings linked to specific memories
  • Easing the shame that keeps people stuck and silent
  • Strengthening a steadier, more self-compassionate sense of identity
  • Supporting the relapse-prevention work already underway

EMDR alongside addiction therapy

EMDR is not a replacement for addiction treatment, and it is not a quick fix. It works best as one part of a wider plan that may include individual therapy, support for the people who love you, and practical recovery tools. We use person-first, non-shaming language throughout, because addiction is not the whole person.

We also move carefully. Reprocessing trauma can be activating, so we make sure stability and support are in place first, and we pace the work to protect your safety.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can EMDR help with addiction?
EMDR can help by addressing the trauma, triggers, and shame that often sit underneath addiction. It is used alongside addiction therapy, not as a standalone cure.
How does EMDR fit into recovery?
It works with the underlying experiences that drive compulsive patterns, which can support the recovery work you are already doing.
Can EMDR reduce cravings and triggers?
Often it can reduce the emotional charge of specific memories tied to triggers, which some people find eases cravings. Results vary from person to person.
Is EMDR a replacement for addiction treatment?
No. EMDR is one part of a wider plan. We combine it with therapy, support, and practical recovery tools.
Is there support for couples or partners too?
Yes. We also offer couples therapy where addiction is present and support for partners and loved ones.
Is EMDR available online?
Yes. Online EMDR is available across Ontario by secure video.

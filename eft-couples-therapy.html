EFT for Couples (Emotionally Focused Therapy)

EFT helps couples understand the painful cycles they get stuck in, and find their way back to safety, trust, and connection.

Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) is a leading, evidence-based approach to couples work. It helps partners understand the painful cycles they get caught in, and find their way back to safety, trust, and genuine connection.

What EFT for couples can help with

  • Disconnection, distance, and feeling like roommates
  • Repeating arguments and conflict cycles
  • Rebuilding trust after betrayal or the impact of addiction
  • Difficulty communicating or feeling heard
  • Growing closer again after a hard season

How EFT works

EFT sees most conflict as protest against lost connection. Together we slow down the cycle that pulls you apart, make sense of the emotions underneath it, and create new conversations that rebuild safety and closeness. The goal is a more secure bond, not just fewer arguments.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is EFT for couples?
Emotionally Focused Therapy is an evidence-based, attachment-focused approach that helps couples break out of negative cycles and rebuild a secure, trusting connection.
Does EFT work?
EFT is one of the most researched couples approaches, with strong evidence for improving relationship satisfaction. Results vary by couple, so we set goals together and review progress.
Can EFT help after betrayal or addiction?
Yes. EFT can support rebuilding trust where safety, honesty, and willingness are present. See also couples therapy where addiction is present.
How many sessions does EFT take?
It varies with where a couple is starting from. We review progress together and move at a pace that feels safe for both partners.
Do both partners need to attend?
EFT is couples work, so sessions usually include both partners. Individual support is also available where it is helpful.
Is EFT available online in Ontario?
Yes. EFT for couples is available by secure video across Ontario, as well as in person.
